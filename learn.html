🫀 What is Organ Donation?

Organ donation is the process of giving an organ or tissue to someone who needs a transplant.

It can happen after death (deceased donation) or while alive (living donation).

Common organs donated include the heart, kidney, liver, lungs, eyes, and pancreas.

👥 Who Can Donate?

Almost anyone can register as a donor, regardless of age, gender, or background.

Medical professionals decide which organs are suitable at the time of donation.

🌱 Living Donation

A healthy person donates one kidney or part of their liver.

🕊️ Deceased Donation

Organs are donated after death with family consent.

🧠 Common Myths vs Facts

❌ "I'm too old to donate."

+

✅ Age doesn't matter; medical condition does.

❌ "My religion doesn't allow it."

+

✅ Most major religions support organ donation.

❌ "Doctors won't try to save me if I'm a donor."

+

✅ Doctors always prioritize saving lives — donation is considered only after death is confirmed.
