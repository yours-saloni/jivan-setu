📚 Frequently Asked Questions

To register as an organ donor, visit our registration page, fill out the required information including your medical history, and submit the form. You'll receive a donor card within 7-10 business days.

Our matching system uses blood type, tissue compatibility, organ size, and medical urgency to find the best matches. Recipients are automatically notified when compatible organs become available.

You'll need a valid government ID, medical records, blood test results, and consent forms. For recipients, additional medical documentation from your treating physician is required.

Hospitals can partner with us by meeting our certification requirements, completing the partnership application, and undergoing our quality assessment process. Contact our hospital relations team for more details.
